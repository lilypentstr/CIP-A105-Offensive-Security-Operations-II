## Activity brief
### Instructor briefing
CTF 2 – Operation Black Forge is the second and final practical offensive-security assessment for Offensive Security Operations II.

You are required to conduct an independent, authorised security assessment against the Academy-issued OPFOR-02 target provided through the ICDFA-controlled OneDrive repository.

The exercise assesses your ability to perform systematic reconnaissance, service and application enumeration, vulnerability analysis, controlled exploitation, post-exploitation, privilege escalation, evidence preservation, risk analysis and professional reporting.

Unlike a guided laboratory, no exploitation path, target credentials, vulnerability list, flag locations or privilege-escalation method will be supplied. You are expected to derive your methodology from evidence and demonstrate sound technical judgement throughout the operation.

Before beginning the assessment, read the complete Operations Brief and Rules of Engagement. All activity must be conducted only against the supplied OPFOR-02 virtual machine within an isolated Internal Network or Host-Only laboratory environment. Bridged networking, public exposure and testing of any external system are strictly prohibited.

Target download: Use only the Health / OPFOR-02 link contained in the official assessment brief. Do not obtain another copy of the target from public repositories or third-party sources.

Students must not use shared credentials, copied exploitation paths or another learner's screenshots. General technical documentation, vendor documentation and legitimate vulnerability research may be consulted where appropriate.

Your final submission must contain sufficient evidence to demonstrate the full operation, including your methodology, attack-surface analysis, confirmed findings, exploitation evidence, post-exploitation and privilege-escalation analysis, mission objectives, risk assessment, remediation recommendations and operator activity log.

Submit a professional assessment package containing the required technical report and supporting evidence in accordance with the full assessment brief.
