## Activity brief
### Instructor briefing
CTF 1 – Operation Iron Raven is an individual, authorised black-box offensive security assessment conducted only against the Academy-issued OPFOR-01 virtual machine inside an isolated laboratory environment.

You are required to independently discover the target, enumerate its attack surface, identify and validate exploitable weaknesses, obtain initial access, conduct controlled post-exploitation and privilege-escalation analysis, recover all authorised mission artifacts encountered, and produce a professional evidence-based security assessment report.

The assessment brief contains the complete Rules of Engagement, laboratory configuration requirements, operational phases, reporting structure, assessment rubric and submission requirements. Read the entire brief before commencing the exercise.

Do not search for or use another learner's attack path. The exercise is designed to assess your own technical reasoning and evidence collection. The original directive explicitly prohibits target-specific walkthroughs and sharing credentials, flags, screenshots or exploitation paths.

Your submission must demonstrate the complete offensive-security workflow, including reconnaissance, attack-surface enumeration, vulnerability analysis, initial access, post-exploitation, privilege escalation, evidence preservation and remediation recommendations. These are the principal assessment areas in the official 100-mark rubric.

All offensive activity must remain strictly within the supplied OPFOR-01 target and the approved isolated lab network. Activity against any external or production system is prohibited.
